Our paper [**"Significant Rural Contributions to Severe Coastal Urban Flooding Through Flow Connectivity"**](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026GL122550) was featured as a Research Spotlight on *Eos*, the science news magazine of AGU.

The spotlight highlights how topography in surrounding rural areas plays a dominant role in funneling water toward coastal cities during hurricane events, and how coastal wetlands help absorb storm surge and protect urban areas.
