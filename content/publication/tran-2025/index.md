abstract: CAMELSH provides a large-sample hourly hydrometeorological dataset and watershed
  attributes at watershed-scale for the contiguous United States (CONUS), extending
  the CAMELS dataset to hourly temporal resolution with additional meteorological
  forcing variables. The dataset supports machine learning and large-sample hydrology
  research for streamflow prediction, flood forecasting, and hydrological modeling
  across diverse watersheds.
